# Year-by-year guide

[English](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-maths-resources-for-kids-australia) · [简体中文](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-maths-resources-for-kids-australia-zh) · [한국어](https://github.com/Airbotix-AI/free-maths-resources-for-kids-australia-ko)

Age/year links and topic order are editorial starting points, not entry-age rules or a certified curriculum map. State cut-offs, readiness and school courses vary. US Grade and UK Key Stage are not automatic Australian equivalents. Foundation has different local names. Years 11–12 depend on the actual subject and state syllabus.

| Australian stage | Approx. age | Suggested focus | Resources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preschool | 3–5 | Counting small sets and comparing shapes | [Khan Academy Kids](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids) · [Topmarks](https://www.topmarks.co.uk/maths-games/3-5-years/counting) · [NRICH](https://nrich.maths.org/) |
| Foundation | 5–6 | Number, matching and part–whole ideas | [Mathematics Hub](https://www.mathematicshub.edu.au/families/) · [reSolve](https://resolve.edu.au/) · [Khan Academy Kids](https://www.khanacademy.org/kids) · [The Math Learning Center apps](https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps) |
| Year 1 | 6–7 | Addition/subtraction and everyday measurement | [Mathematics Hub](https://www.mathematicshub.edu.au/families/) · [AMSI Calculate](https://calculate.org.au/category/student-parent-resources/) · [The Math Learning Center apps](https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps) |
| Year 2 | 7–8 | Place value, arrays and equal groups | [reSolve](https://resolve.edu.au/) · [AMSI Calculate](https://calculate.org.au/category/student-parent-resources/) · [The Math Learning Center apps](https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps) |
| Year 3 | 8–9 | Multiplication and beginning fractions | [reSolve](https://resolve.edu.au/) · [NRICH](https://nrich.maths.org/) · [The Math Learning Center apps](https://www.mathlearningcenter.org/apps) · [PhET maths simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/filter?subjects=math&type=html) |

| Year 5 | 10–11 | Fraction operations, decimals and data | [reSolve](https://resolve.edu.au/) · [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) · [Mathigon / Polypad](https://mathigon.org/) |
| Year 6 | 11–12 | Fraction/decimal/percentage connections | [Mathematics Hub](https://www.mathematicshub.edu.au/families/) · [NRICH](https://nrich.maths.org/) · [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) · [Mathigon / Polypad](https://mathigon.org/) |

| Year 10 | 15–16 | Functions, modelling and statistics | [reSolve](https://resolve.edu.au/) · [Desmos calculators](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) · [GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/calculator) |
| Year 11 | 16–17 | School-course-specific functions and algebra | [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) · [Desmos calculators](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) · [GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/calculator) · [NRICH](https://nrich.maths.org/) |
| Year 12 | 17–18 | Course-specific calculus, probability or statistics | [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) · [Desmos calculators](https://www.desmos.com/calculator) · [GeoGebra](https://www.geogebra.org/calculator) · [NRICH](https://nrich.maths.org/) |

This edition is in English. External resources have their own language support; translating this guide does not translate their lessons. International resources are selected by topic, not assumed Australian curriculum alignment.

Official sources support the access notes. No child accounts, paid subscriptions, full courses or every interactive were tested. Start with one model, an explanation and a check.
