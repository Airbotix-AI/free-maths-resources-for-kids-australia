# Year 8: area and volume

Choose the shape. Identify perpendicular height. Use matching units, then check your answer.

## 1. Rectangle

**Rule:** A = l × w

**Symbols:** l = length; w = width; A = area.

**Use when:** Find the area inside a rectangle.

**Worked example:** 8 cm × 5 cm = 40 cm²

**Common mistake:** Perimeter measures the edge, not area.

## 2. Triangle

**Rule:** A = ½ × b × h

**Symbols:** b = base; h = perpendicular height.

**Use when:** Use any base with its matching height.

**Worked example:** ½ × 10 cm × 6 cm = 30 cm²

**Common mistake:** The sloping side is not the height.

## 3. Parallelogram

**Rule:** A = b × h

**Symbols:** b = base; h = perpendicular height.

**Use when:** Opposite sides are parallel.

**Worked example:** 9 cm × 4 cm = 36 cm²

**Common mistake:** Measure height at a right angle to b.

## 4. Trapezium

**Rule:** A = ½ × (a + b) × h

**Symbols:** a, b = parallel sides; h = their distance.

**Use when:** Average the parallel sides, then × height.

**Worked example:** ½ × (6 + 10) × 4 = 32 cm²

**Common mistake:** a and b must be the parallel sides.

## 5. Circle

**Rule:** A = π × r²

**Symbols:** r = radius; diameter = 2r; π ≈ 3.14159.

**Use when:** Find the area inside a circle.

**Worked example:** r = 3 cm: A = 9π ≈ 28.27 cm²

**Common mistake:** Halve the diameter before squaring.

## 6. Right prism

**Rule:** V = B × L

**Symbols:** B = cross-section area; L = prism length.

**Use when:** The cross-section stays the same.

**Worked example:** B = 12 cm², L = 5 cm: V = 60 cm³

**Common mistake:** B is an area; L is a perpendicular length.

## Check before finishing

Lengths: same unit. Area: square units (cm²). Volume: cubic units (cm³). Round only at the end.

## Challenge and complete solution

A triangular prism has base 8 cm, perpendicular triangle height 5 cm and prism length 12 cm. Find its volume and explain the units.

Base area = ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 cm². Volume = 20 × 12 = 240 cm³.
